Delete your account

How to delete your What Should We Watch account and the data that goes with it, with the app or without it.

In the app

  1. Open What Should We Watch and tap You.
  2. Tap your name, or Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Without the app

Email wsww.app@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it.

What’s deleted

Your account, and the data on our servers that goes with it:

  • your decisions
  • your watched history
  • your taste notes
  • your Friends activity

It is deleted permanently within 60 days.

What isn’t

Feedback you sent from the app and our diagnostic logs aren’t linked to your name or email. They are kept only as long as we need them to run and improve the app.

Used it as a guest?

Guests have no account. Logging out clears the app’s data from your phone. What you swiped stays on our servers under a random identifier that isn’t linked to your name or email, so we can’t find it to delete it.

Questions

Email wsww.app@gmail.com. More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
